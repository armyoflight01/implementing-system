Sponsorship Transfer (Kafala) & Ministry Quotas

Package Expiration & Sponsorship Transfer (Kafala) Regulations: Upon expiration of promotional discounted packages (e.g. 3-month package @ 11,000 SAR), clients must be notified of their eligibility to transfer the worker's sponsorship. Crucial Rule: The Ministry of Labor has established statutory caps on the number of sponsorship transfers allowed for each nationality, which cannot be exceeded.
